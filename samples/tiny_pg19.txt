It was a quiet evening, and the old library had already closed its heavy doors
to the street. In the reading room, however, one lamp remained lit. A student
sat beneath it with a stack of books, turning pages slowly and copying a few
sentences into a notebook. The silence made every sound distinct: the scratch of
the pen, the clock above the shelves, and the distant rain against the windows.

The story he was reading began in a city by the sea. Its people spoke often of
ships, weather, trade, and rumor. Every family seemed to know someone who had
left home and returned changed, or who had not returned at all. The young
protagonist listened to these stories with a mixture of fear and longing, and
wondered whether knowledge could be found without first becoming lost.
